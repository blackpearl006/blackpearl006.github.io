At **Niramai Health Analytix** (Bengaluru), I work on **Thermalytix** — an AI-powered breast-cancer screening solution that combines thermal imaging with machine learning. The approach is **radiation-free, non-contact, and painless**, and works across women of all ages and breast densities, making early screening more accessible.

The pipeline analyzes hundreds of thousands of temperature points across a thermal scan and produces a quantitative **breast-health score** to help clinicians flag abnormal regions for follow-up.
