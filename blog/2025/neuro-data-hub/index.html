An open-access platform designed to help you explore, compare, and analyze neuroimaging datasets. This site brings together an extensive collection of 50 carefully curated public datasets, totaling over 50,000 brain scans.

NeuroDataHub — your gateway to the world’s largest open-access collection of brain imaging datasets*. Whether you’re a neuroscientist, machine learning researcher, clinician, or student, NeuroDataHub makes it easy to find, understand, and use over 50 high-quality neuroimaging datasets spanning 50,000+ brain scans.
From structural MRI (T1-weighted) and functional MRI (fMRI) to diffusion-weighted imaging (DWI), cerebral blood flow (CBF), and resting-state data, we bring together richly annotated studies covering diverse populations, age groups, and scanner types — all through a clean, searchable interface.

In the age of data-driven neuroscience, accessing and comparing neuroimaging datasets across cohorts and scanners remains an uphill challenge. Datasets are scattered, inconsistently formatted, and difficult to navigate for non-experts. This lack of interoperability and discoverability slows down reproducibility, cross-study learning, and progress toward generalizable models of the human brain.

That’s why I created NeuroDataHub — an open-access platform for exploring, comparing, and analyzing neuroimaging datasets at scale. With over 50 curated datasets and growing, NeuroDataHub is designed to bridge the gap between raw neuroimaging data and accessible, actionable insights for both researchers and developers.

What’s Inside?

NeuroDataHub is not a repository — it’s a meta-platform. It doesn’t host the raw data; instead, it provides streamlined access to metadata, standardized representations, and downloadable links where applicable. Key features include:

1. 🧩 Dataset Overview Cards: Each dataset is indexed with rich metadata: modalities (T1w, ASL, fMRI, DWI), age range, population type (clinical, healthy), scanner info, sample size, and references.

2. 🔍 Search & Filter Interface**: Looking for a dataset with ASL imaging in older adults scanned on a Siemens 3T? The advanced search tool makes it trivial.

3. 📊 Compare Datasets: Visually compare distributions of age, sex, modality coverage, or site diversity across datasets.

4. 🧠 Dataset Gallery Views: Toggle between table and grid layouts — optimized for either quick scanning or deep inspection of each dataset’s details.

📚 Curated Studies for Every Dataset

For every dataset on NeuroDataHub, we’ve identified two highly relevant published studies — carefully selected to reflect impactful, well-documented use cases. This feature serves as both a launchpad for beginners and a reference point for experienced researchers.

  • 🧪 For new researchers and students, these studies provide a concrete starting point. You’ll see how real-world research questions were framed, what preprocessing or machine learning methods were used, and how results were interpreted. It’s like getting a guided tour of what’s possible with the data.
  • 🧠 For seasoned scientists, these studies offer context and benchmarks. Quickly assess how a dataset has been used before, identify gaps or extensions for your own work, or replicate existing pipelines for validation and comparison.

Each study is directly linked, summarized, and matched to the dataset’s strengths — whether that’s longitudinal aging, resting-state connectivity, diffusion metrics, or disease progression.

💡 This way, NeuroDataHub doesn’t just give you data — it gives you direction.
